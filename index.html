Atlas — Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

Atlas is work software. A company signs up, invites its employees, and uses it to assign tasks, post announcements, keep employee records and manage leave. This policy explains what we hold, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version. We collect what is needed to run the app: your phone number to sign you in, your name so colleagues know who did what, and the records your employer keeps about your employment. Some of those records are sensitive — pay, bank details, identity document numbers — so they are stored separately and only your employer's owner and managers can read them. We do not sell data, we do not advertise, and we do not build profiles of you.

1. Who is responsible

Atlas is operated by Mohamed Awaden, Egypt. Contact: mohmd46alaa@gmail.com.

For data your employer puts into Atlas, your employer is the data controller and we are the processor. Your employer decides which records to keep about you, including whether to record your salary, bank account or identity document numbers. We provide the software and the safeguards; we do not decide what goes in. If you are an employee asking to see, correct or delete an employment record, start with your employer. For your own account, you can act directly — see section 6.

2.1 Employee records

If your employer uses the employee-record features, Atlas also stores the following. All of it is optional and entered by a person — Atlas never reads it from your device, and a company that leaves these fields blank has none of it stored.

How the sensitive fields are protected. Pay, bank details and identity numbers are not stored alongside the rest of your profile. They live in a separate database record that our server-side rules only release to your company's owner and managers. This is not a setting in the app that could be worked around by a modified copy of it — an employee's credentials cannot retrieve another employee's salary from our servers at all. Bank account and national ID numbers are masked to the last four digits wherever they are displayed.

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 classes financial data as sensitive personal data, and we treat identity document numbers the same way.

What we do not collect

  • No GPS or background location
  • No contacts, no call logs, no SMS content
  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps
  • No biometrics, no health data beyond the blood type your employer may record for emergencies
  • No scans or images of identity documents — numbers only, typed in
  • No card numbers. Payment is handled entirely by our payment providers; we receive only a confirmation that a payment succeeded

3. Who can see your data

Inside Atlas, data is isolated per company. A member of one company cannot read another company's data — this is enforced by database rules on our servers, not by the app.

Within your company:

  • Employees see their own tasks, the company feed, their own leave balance and requests, and the non-sensitive part of colleagues' profiles — name, role, work contact, emergency contact.
  • Managers and the owner see all tasks, the team dashboard, leave requests they are asked to approve, and the sensitive records described in section 2.1.
  • You can always see and correct your own contact details, emergency contact and blood type.
  • Posts marked "managers only" are withheld from employees by the database.

4. Who we share with

We do not sell data. We use these processors:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication, database, file storage, push notifications, crash reporting. See section 9 for where this is stored.
  • Paddle.com Market Ltd — payment processing and merchant of record for customers outside Egypt. They receive the amount and the company owner's billing contact details.
  • Paymob — payment processing for customers in Egypt. They receive the amount and the company owner's billing contact details.

Neither payment provider receives employee data. Billing is between us and the company owner; the number of paid seats is the only thing about your workforce that reaches them, and it is a number, not a list.

We disclose data to authorities only where legally required, and will tell the affected company unless the law forbids it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Active company: for as long as the company uses Atlas.
  • Cancelled subscription: data is kept read-only for 90 days so nothing is lost by accident, then deleted.
  • You leave, or are removed from, a company: your employment record — including the sensitive fields in section 2.1 — is deleted at that moment.
  • Deleted account: removed immediately, see below.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.

Your employer may be required by employment or tax law to keep payroll and identity records for a number of years after you leave. Atlas does not keep them for your employer — if they need to, they keep their own copy outside Atlas, and what they do with it is governed by their own policy.

6. Your rights

Under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 — and the GDPR where it applies — you may access, correct, delete, or export your data, and object to processing.

Correct your own details, from inside the app

Open your profile and edit your personal phone, email, home address, emergency contact and blood type. You do not need to ask anyone. The employment and payroll fields are your employer's record, so ask them to correct those.

Delete your account, from inside the app

Settings → Account → Delete account. This erases your profile, your contact and emergency details, your leave records, your membership and your payroll and identity record — salary, bank account, national ID and passport number are deleted with everything else. Work you completed stays with the company, because it belongs to your employer's business records; your name is removed from it.

Export everything

A company owner can export all company data as JSON from Settings → Your data → Export everything. The export includes the private payroll and identity records, so it is a complete answer to an access request — and should be handled with the same care as the data itself.

For anything else, email mohmd46alaa@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

7. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS); data is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud.
  • Access is enforced server-side by database rules, so a modified copy of the app cannot bypass it.
  • Sensitive records are held in a separate database document that those rules release only to your company's owner and managers, so they cannot be exposed by a mistake in the app's screens.
  • Bank account and national ID numbers are masked to the last four digits wherever they are shown.
  • Sign-in is by SMS code to your own phone number. There is no password to be reused or leaked.
  • We never see or store card details.
  • If a breach affects you, we will notify affected companies and the Egyptian Data Protection Centre as the law requires.

8. Children

Atlas is for workplaces and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Where your data is stored, and international transfers

Atlas runs on Google Cloud. Our application servers are in the European Union (europe-west1). Our database is in Google's United States multi-region, and photos you attach are stored in the European Union.

So if you use Atlas from Egypt or the EU, your data is transferred to and stored in the United States and the European Union. These transfers rely on Google Cloud's Standard Contractual Clauses, and Google Cloud is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Data is encrypted in transit between every one of these locations.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will notify company owners by email and show a notice in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
